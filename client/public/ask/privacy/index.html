问问立正隐私政策

更新于2026年10月4日

English version

问问立正是立正（Yuzheng Sun）做的AI问答工具，包括网页ask.lizheng.ai、lizheng.ai主页上的问答区，以及问问立正iPhone App。App里显示的就是同一个网页，适用同一份政策。

公开问答与同意

问问立正用公开的文章、视频和课程文字稿回答问题。你提问时，同意保存和公开使用问题、完整回答与出处，帮助有同样问题的人，并用于改进回答和内容选题。常见问答会去掉个人信息后精选展示；你也可以分享问答，公开页面能被搜索找到。只写你愿意公开的内容。公开使用以提问时展示且你同意的说明为准。

我们保存什么、为什么

  • 提问和回答。你的提问、AI生成的完整回答和所用出处会保存下来，用来改进回答、了解大家关心什么，立正也会从中找写文章、做视频的选题。常见的问答去掉个人信息后，可能公开在「别人在问什么」。每条公开的问答有自己的网页，搜索引擎也能找到；页面上显示大约什么时候问的（精确到5分钟）。你也可以分享自己的问答：分享后它有自己的网页，显示问题和回答，不显示你填的处境原文，搜索引擎也能找到。回答可能引用处境中的细节，请在分享前检查。
  • 「结合我的处境」。你填写的处境会和提问一起发给AI模型，只用来生成这次回答；我们不保存处境原文，也不单独公开它。回答可能引用其中的细节，并随完整回答保存、在上述范围内公开使用。
  • 匿名使用统计。随机生成的浏览器标识和对话标识、这是第几轮、问题字数、问法和入口，用来了解使用情况。
  • 每天的次数。浏览器里一个签名的随机标识，用来计算你今天问了几次；同一网络的免费提问总数按网络地址的加密摘要计数。
  • 验证Founding身份（可选）。你输入的邮箱用来发送验证码，并向超线性学院社区核验你是不是Founding Member。登录状态加密保存，最长12小时。
  • 点赞（可选）。验证身份后点「有帮助」，会用邮箱的加密摘要记下你点过哪些公开问答，只用来避免重复计数。
  • 访问统计。页面访问和按钮点击的匿名计数（比如点开了哪一类出处），以及每次打开看了多久、滑到哪里、有没有看到和点开「别人在问什么」、分享了几次、隔几天有没有再来。这些按天合计成数字，用的是随机的浏览器标识和第一次来的日期，不含提问内容。

我们不做的事

  • 提问记录不关联邮箱、账号或IP。登录信息不和提问记在一起，也不交给AI模型。但你输入的文字仍可能包含能识别个人的信息，发送给AI前不会自动去掉；请别填写私密信息。
  • 不保存原始IP，不收集位置、通讯录或广告标识，不做跨App跟踪，不出售数据，也不用于广告。
  • App只在你选择保存图片时把回答长图写入相册，不读取相册。

谁会处理这些数据

  • Builder Space（space.ai-builders.com）：运行问答应用，并通过AI网关转发下面两类请求。AI网关只记录模型名、token数、费用估算、时间戳和工具调用次数等用量元数据，不保存问题和回答的正文；这与本产品在Upstash保存问答记录是两回事。
  • DeepSeek（官方API，api.deepseek.com）：当前使用deepseek-v4-flash生成回答。它接收当前问题、开启后填写的处境、最近最多6轮的问题与回答摘要，以及检索到的公开资料和分析材料。核对或修复回答时，生成的回答也会再次交给它处理。常见问答的公开整理使用同一回答AI，接收问题、完整回答和主题，去掉个人信息、挑选和整理。
  • OpenAI：使用text-embedding-3-small匹配公开资料，接收由当前问题、处境以及必要时的上一轮问题组成的检索文本；不接收完整对话摘要。公开资料的向量也由它生成，用于匹配。
  • Vercel：网站托管和访问统计。
  • Upstash：保存次数、登录状态、问答记录和访问统计。
  • Resend：发送验证码邮件。
  • Circle（超线性学院社区）：核验Founding Member身份。

邮箱、登录信息和账号身份不交给上述AI模型。输入原文在AI处理前不会自动去掉个人信息。OpenAI的API数据规则规定，默认不将API数据用于训练，显式选择共享除外；滥用监控日志通常最多保留30天。DeepSeek通用隐私政策说明，它不覆盖开发者应用中终端用户的数据处理。我们尚未取得当前API适用的训练、保存和删除承诺，不保证其API数据默认不用于训练。

App中的AI同意

在App里，第一次AI提问前会单独展示公开用途、发送的数据、Builder Space及实际AI接收方。只有点「同意AI处理与公开使用」才会发送；点「不同意」不发送，也不扣提问次数，仍可浏览公开问答和出处。披露、接收方或回答模型变化时会重新征求同意。

可以在「说明」里点「撤回AI数据发送同意」。撤回后，新提问和重新生成回答都需要再次同意。撤回不收回已经发出的内容，也不自动删除已有记录；如需删除，请按下文联系我们。

保存多久

  • 问答记录保存到立正删除为止；公开的问答随时可以撤下，撤下后它的网页大约十分钟内失效，搜索引擎下次来抓取时会把它移除。
  • 次数记录两天后自动删除；验证码10分钟内有效；登录状态最长12小时。
  • 按天的访问统计400天后删除；浏览器里记的第一次来的日期400天后过期。

你可以

  • 不登录直接使用，每天3次。
  • 只写你愿意交给AI处理并公开使用的内容。「结合我的处境」默认关闭。
  • 要求删除：记录不关联身份，我们没法按人查找。如果想删除自己问过的问题，或撤下公开的问答，请写信告诉我们大致时间和问题内容（分享过的，附上链接就行），我们找到后删除本产品记录、撤下页面。已发给AI的数据按对应服务商的规则处理；已经公开的内容可能被他人保存。

儿童

问问立正不面向13岁以下的儿童。

变更与联系

政策有变化时，我们会更新本页和上面的日期。有任何问题，请写信到yz@superlinear.academy。

Privacy Policy

Updated October 4, 2026

问问立正 (Ask Lizheng) is an AI question-and-answer tool made by Yuzheng Sun (立正). It covers the website ask.lizheng.ai, the Q&A section on the lizheng.ai homepage, and the 问问立正 iPhone app. The app shows the same web page, so the same policy applies.

Public Q&A and permission

Ask Lizheng answers questions using public articles, videos and course transcripts. Asking means agreeing to save and use the question, complete answer and sources publicly, to help others, improve answers and find content topics. Common questions are selected for display with personal details removed. You can also share an answer on a public page that search engines can find. Write only what you are willing to make public. Public use follows the disclosure shown and accepted when you ask.

What we keep, and why

  • Questions and answers. Your question, the complete AI answer and the sources it used are kept to improve answers, to learn what people care about, and to find topics for essays and videos. With personal details removed, common questions and answers may be published in “别人在问什么” (What others ask). Each published question has its own web page that search engines can show, with roughly when it was asked (to five minutes). You can also share your own question: it then gets its own page showing the question and the answer, without a separate copy of the situation you wrote, and search engines can show it. The answer may quote details from your situation; check it before sharing.
  • Your situation (optional). If you turn on “结合我的处境”, what you write is sent to the AI model with your question, only to produce this answer. We don't keep the situation text or publish it separately. The answer may quote its details, which are saved with the complete answer and used publicly within the scope above.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. Random browser and conversation identifiers, the turn number, question length, the way of asking and the entry point.
  • Daily limit. A signed random identifier in your browser counts today's questions; free questions from the same network are counted by a keyed hash of the network address.
  • Founding Member verification (optional). The email you enter receives a one-time code and is checked against the Superlinear Academy community for Founding Member status. The signed-in state is kept encrypted for up to 12 hours.
  • Likes (optional). After verifying, tapping “有帮助” (helpful) records which published answers you liked under a keyed hash of your email, only to avoid double counting.
  • Visit statistics. Anonymous counts of page views and button taps (for example, which kind of source was opened), how long each visit lasted, how far the page was scrolled, whether “别人在问什么” was seen or opened, how often answers were shared, and whether the browser came back on a later day. They are totalled by day using a random browser identifier and the date of the first visit, never the question text.

What we don't do

  • Question records are not linked to an email, account or IP address. Sign-in details are never stored with questions or sent to the AI model. However, the text you enter may identify you and is not automatically redacted before AI processing. Please do not enter private information.
  • We don't keep raw IP addresses, collect location, contacts or advertising identifiers, track you across apps, sell data or use it for advertising.
  • The app writes an answer image to your photo library only when you choose to save it, and never reads your photos.

Who processes the data

  • Builder Space (space.ai-builders.com) hosts the Q&A application and forwards the two types of requests below through its AI gateway. The gateway keeps usage metadata such as model names, token counts, cost estimates, timestamps and tool-call counts, without storing question or answer bodies. This is separate from this product's Q&A archive in Upstash.
  • DeepSeek (official API, api.deepseek.com) currently generates answers using deepseek-v4-flash. It receives the current question, enabled situation, questions and answer summaries from up to six recent turns, and retrieved public sources and analysis material. Generated answers may be sent back for validation or repair. The same answer AI prepares common Q&A for public display, receiving the question, complete answer and topics to remove personal details, select and organize them.
  • OpenAI matches public sources using text-embedding-3-small. It receives search text composed of the current question, situation and, when needed, the previous question, rather than complete conversation summaries. It also generates vectors for the public source material used in matching.
  • Vercel hosts the website and counts visits.
  • Upstash stores daily counts, sign-in state, question records and visit statistics.
  • Resend sends verification emails.
  • Circle (the Superlinear Academy community) confirms Founding Member status.

Email, sign-in details and account identities are not sent to these AI models. Input is not automatically redacted before AI processing. OpenAI's API data rules exclude API data from training by default unless explicitly shared; abuse-monitoring logs are normally retained for up to 30 days. DeepSeek's general privacy policy says it does not cover end-user data processing in developers' applications. We have not yet obtained the training, retention and deletion commitments applicable to the API in use, and do not guarantee that its API data is excluded from training by default.

AI permission in the app

Before the first AI question in the app, a separate dialog lists the public uses, data, Builder Space and actual receiving AI services. Data is sent only after you tap “同意AI处理与公开使用” (Agree to AI processing and public use). “不同意” (Do not agree) sends nothing and does not use your daily allowance; you can still browse public answers and sources. Changes to the disclosure, recipients or answer model require fresh permission.

In “说明” (About), tap “撤回AI数据发送同意” to withdraw permission. New questions and answer retries then require permission again. Withdrawal cannot recall data already sent and does not automatically delete existing records. Contact us as described below to request deletion.

How long we keep it

  • Question records are kept until the owner deletes them; published questions can be withdrawn at any time. A withdrawn question's page stops working within about ten minutes, and search engines drop it when they next visit.
  • Daily counts are deleted after two days; verification codes expire after 10 minutes; sign-in lasts at most 12 hours.
  • Visit statistics by day are deleted after 400 days; the first-visit date kept in your browser expires after 400 days.

Your choices

  • Use it without signing in, 3 answers a day.
  • Write only what you are willing to have processed by AI and used publicly. “结合我的处境” is off by default.
  • Deletion: records aren't linked to identities, so we can't look them up by person. Email us the approximate time and wording of your question (or its shared link), and we will find and delete the product's record and take down its page. Data already sent to AI follows the receiving service's rules; others may have saved content that was already public.

Children

问问立正 is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes. Questions: yz@superlinear.academy.
