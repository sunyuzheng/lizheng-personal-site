## The job of the personal site

The personal site is neither a complete philosophy archive nor a catalogue of four Superlinear products. It should leave a visitor with one association and four supporting inferences:

> **Yuzheng Sun is the person who names, explains, and builds under `MAKE WHAT LASTS. / 做出你的代表作。`**

1. He recognizes the difference between output and a work that carries a maker's judgment, craft, and responsibility.
2. This is a real personal wager, not a virtue he has awarded himself.
3. His career, public record, books, conversations, products, and institution show unusual range and present capability.
4. Superlinear Academy is the most important long-term work he is betting on; whether it ultimately earns the idea remains for its work and time to answer.

The page should make these conclusions feel obvious without repeatedly telling the visitor that Yuzheng is impressive or already belongs beside the people the belief honors.

## The desired emotional sequence

1. **Recognition:** “I do not want my life to disappear into tasks that reset.”
2. **Aspiration:** “A person's judgment and craft really can become work the world keeps choosing.”
3. **Respect:** “He can recognize and explain that standard, and the people and work around him suggest unusual range.”
4. **Credibility:** “He has made consequential choices, left public judgments, taught, built, published, and operated in reality.”
5. **Possibility:** “His largest current wager is unfinished; there is a place for my own unfinished work here too.”

## Authority by imputation

The page uses four different kinds of evidence for four different jobs:

- **Exemplary makers and works set the horizon.** Jobs, Zhang Xiaolong, Messi, Nolan, Hinton, Buffett, and Tsunekazu Nishioka show the height of the aspiration. They do not endorse Yuzheng or Superlinear.
- **Yuzheng's choices establish sincerity and stake.** His movement from externally scored success toward work of his own explains why he means the idea.
- **Current work establishes capability.** AI Builders, the free community, the membership, enterprise programs, books, public writing, and conversations show that the belief has already entered real products and relationships.
- **Works and time retain the verdict.** Superlinear is described as the most important long-term work Yuzheng is betting on, not as a self-certified masterpiece.

## Homepage sequence

### 1. Hero — one memory, one wager

- Keep the full Acquired photograph so Yuzheng, Ben Gilbert, and David Rosenthal all remain visible.
- Use the bilingual brand command as the only headline.
- Explain the human meaning in two sentences, then name Superlinear as the long-term wager.
- Keep credentials as a quiet line under the calls to action; they create authority without becoming the proposition.

### 2. Desire and horizon — why this matters

- Begin with the capable person's fear: a life of successful work that never truly becomes theirs.
- Name AI's present effect without turning the page into an AI trend essay: first versions are abundant; discovery, standards, craft, authorship, and continued value are not.
- Honor concrete people through the things inseparable from their names: products, football, films, non-consensus convictions that survived decades, and temple craft that gave old timber another thousand years. Do not divide them into consulting-style virtue cards.
- Compress the intellectual structure into one sentence about people shaping work and work shaping people. The homepage should not teach the complete maker–work–world–time architecture.

### 3. Personal path — why he means it

- Use Cornell, Amazon/Meta, Tencent, Statsig, and Superlinear as formative environments, not a logo wall.
- Make one consequential transition visible: becoming good at externally scored games did not answer where his best attention should accumulate.
- Do not ask the story to prove the universal belief or place Yuzheng beside the exemplary figures.

### 4. Public judgment — leave something inspectable

- Keep one dated record: the February 2023 ChatGPT essay written before GPT-4.
- It demonstrates willingness to reason publicly before the outcome was obvious. It is not a claim of permanent foresight.

### 5. Superlinear — reveal the institution and the wager

- Say directly that Superlinear is Yuzheng's most important long-term work.
- State the institutional ambition before the product map in ordinary language: build a real academy where people with professional depth can turn judgment into products, work, and ventures. Do not turn the ambition into a comprehensive mission-statement list.
- Present the free community, AI Builders, the membership, and enterprise work through the distinct change each enables. Keep complete sales arguments on their own sites.
- Keep Yage visible as the long-term teaching partner, and make his technical authority legible through his Columbia doctorate, AI research, paper record, and leading-conference work rather than a self-awarded label; use the real image of Yuzheng and Yage together.

### 6. Conversations, books, and informed voices — quiet proof

- Conversations show whom Yuzheng chooses to learn from and honor, as well as the quality of his network and inputs.
- Books show judgment given a form that can work without the author in the room.
- Testimonials let informed outsiders interpret the work.

### 7. Final invitation — return authorship to the visitor

End on the strongest bridge between aspiration and action:

> **Your defining work will not arrive finished.**
>
> **你的代表作，不会一开始就是代表作。**

The primary invitation is the free community. AI Builders and direct collaboration remain secondary routes.

## Surface boundaries

| Surface | Primary effect | Use of the mother idea |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | create the Yuzheng ↔ idea association and reveal the long-term wager | primary memory and narrative spine |
| About page | answer factual “who is this?” questions | one compact belief paragraph; no manifesto |
| Creator collaboration page | make a host want to invite Yuzheng | use the idea as an interesting conversation territory, then show concrete episode evidence |
| SEO / structured data / `llms.txt` | make identity and relationships legible to machines | precise, compressed, non-promotional wording |
| Social profiles | win recognition in a few seconds and route the right person onward | line one or first clause; platform-specific proof and CTA follow |

## Language standard

- Prefer concrete verbs, real nouns, people, works, dates, choices, and consequences.
- Do not use balanced abstract lists merely because they sound comprehensive.
- Do not turn every paragraph into a slogan or every idea into a card.
- In Chinese, use natural compact mixed writing: `AI时代`, `20,000+成员`, `Superlinear Academy创始人`.
- Avoid defensive disclaimers on audience-facing surfaces. Preserve exact boundaries in factual pages, structured data, and source documentation.
